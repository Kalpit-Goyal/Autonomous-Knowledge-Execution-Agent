# Billing and Refunds

Meridian Cloud bills in advance, monthly or annually. Annual plans are discounted roughly two months free, which is why they carry different refund terms than monthly plans.

## Refund windows

A customer may request a refund within 30 days of the charge date. Monthly plans are refunded pro rata by unused whole days.

Annual subscriptions are non-refundable after 14 days, because the annual discount is amortised across the full term and cannot be cleanly reversed. This clause is specific to annual billing and supersedes the 30-day window for annual plans only.

Refund requests received after the applicable window are declined automatically. A support agent may offer account credit instead, at their discretion, but only with supervisor sign-off.

## Partial refunds

Partial refunds up to and including 250 USD may be issued by a support agent without additional sign-off, provided the ticket is inside its refund window. Anything above 250 USD requires billing supervisor approval.

## Non-refundable items

The following are never refundable, regardless of window or plan:

- Professional services
- Onboarding packages
- Custom integration work
- Training sessions that have already been attended

## Credit notes and chargebacks

Chargebacks are not preventable by support. When a customer opens one, open a P3 ticket, attach the Stripe dispute ID, and do not issue a refund for the same charge - that produces a double credit against the merchant account.

## Plan pricing

Current list pricing is maintained in the product catalog. The Growth plan is 450 USD per month on monthly billing. Annual pricing is published per plan in the catalog and changes with each pricing review, so treat the catalog as authoritative for annual figures.
