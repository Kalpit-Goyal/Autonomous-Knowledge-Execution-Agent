# Plans and Entitlements

Meridian Cloud sells in four plans: Starter, Growth, Scale, and Enterprise. The authoritative price list is the product catalog, not this article - the catalog is updated at each pricing review while this article is not.

## Starter

The entry plan. 5 seats included, 25 GB storage, 100k API calls per month, standard support with a 48-hour response target. No SSO, no dedicated success manager.

## Growth

The mid plan and the most common upgrade target. 25 seats, 250 GB, 2M API calls per month, priority support with a 12-hour response target. No SSO, no dedicated success manager.

## Scale

25 or more active users typically. 100 seats, 1 TB storage, 20M API calls per month, premium support with a 4-hour response target, dedicated success manager, and SSO enabled.

## Enterprise

Everything negotiated. Seats, storage, and call volume are contracted rather than listed. Dedicated support with a 1-hour response target, SSO, a dedicated success manager, and access to raw database dumps through an approved account-team ticket.

## Adding seats

Seats are billed as a prorated line item from the date of the change. Adding up to 50 seats does not need manager approval. Adding more than 50 seats at once requires explicit manager approval, and a seat grant above that threshold cannot be self-served by support.

## Trials

Every new workspace starts on a 14-day trial of Growth. A trial may be extended at most once, by at most 14 days. Any further extension is an account-manager decision, and a trial cannot be extended beyond a total of 28 days from creation.

## Usage limits

Exceeding a usage limit throttles rather than blocks. API calls beyond the monthly allowance are rate limited, and storage beyond the allowance stops new uploads while leaving reads working. Throttling is reversible by upgrading the plan.
