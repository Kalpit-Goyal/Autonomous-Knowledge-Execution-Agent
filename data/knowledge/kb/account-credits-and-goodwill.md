# Account Credits and Goodwill

Account credits are a discretionary instrument. They are the right answer when a customer was let down by us and a refund would be the wrong shape of remedy - for example a service incident that self-resolved, or a support response that missed its SLA.

## Authorisation

Support agents may issue goodwill credit for a documented service failure on a plan with a committed SLA, up to the value of one month of the plan fee, and only when the ticket is P1 or P2.

Service credits are discretionary. Agents may not issue service credits without supervisor approval regardless of ticket value. If the customer is asking for compensation because of a missed SLA, that is a service credit and it needs supervisor approval.

Credits above one month of plan fee, and every credit tied to a data-loss claim, require account-manager approval. Credits tied to a suspected security incident are never issued by support - they are handled by the security team under the incident process.

## Recording a credit

Every credit must record the ticket that justifies it, the incident or failure description, the approving human, and the expiry. Credits expire after 12 months of issue and are not transferable.

## Goodwill versus refund

Prefer a refund when the product genuinely did not match what was sold and the customer can prove it. Prefer a credit when the product works and the failure was in our service. A credit is not a substitute for an SLA commitment - a missed SLA is escalated, not compensated silently.
